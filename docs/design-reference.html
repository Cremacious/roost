Border rule · the one-slab principle

The flagship fix for #107. The 4px slab bottom-border is what gives a card its lift, so it belongs to one element: the card. When a bordered card also gives every child row its own 4px slab, the horizontal lines stack and the surface looks unfinished. This is exactly what the /money "Who owes who" card does today.

Only the card carries the slab bottom. Rows are separated by a single 1px divider (border). Calmer, and the card still reads as one object.

The rule: a container that already has a border/slab must not give its children a second heavy border. Inside a card, separate rows with a 1px divider (var(--roost-border)). Use the 4px slab bottom only on standalone SlabCards and feature tiles, never on a card and its rows at the same time.

SlabCard

The base card for the whole app: rounded-2xl, a 1.5px border on all sides, and a 4px bottom border that is the only source of the clay lift. Feature cards tint the bottom with their section color; neutral cards use border-bottom. On press: translateY(2px) and the bottom border drops to 2px.

Buttons

Every primary button is a slab: the section color with a 3px darker bottom-border, radius 14 (11 when small). Press drops it 2px (1px small) and thins the bottom border. The page you are on owns the color. Secondary actions use the ghost style; a permission-locked control keeps its color but drops to 55% opacity with a Lock icon.

Left to right: section primaries · small save + ghost cancel · locked (permission gate, 55% opacity + Lock) · FAB.

Badges, counts & inputs

Badges are section-tinted pills (color at 14% fill, 32% border). Count pills are solid section color on white. Inputs are 16px (no iOS zoom), labels are a fixed dark grey #374151 at weight 700, and focus turns the border and bottom-border the section color.

Consistency rules to enforce

What the #107 pass should hold every surface to.

1One slab bottom-border per card. A bordered container never also slabs its child rows: use a 1px divider inside.
2Slab bottom-border is the only 3D device. No left, right, or top border used as a color accent.
3Buttons share one system: section color face, 3px darker bottom, radius 14/11, press translateY. No one-off button styles.
4The page's section color owns its buttons, toggles, focus, badges, completion states. Brand red only on chores, sidebar, and destructive actions.
5Text never below weight 600. Labels #374151/700. Muted text only for captions and timestamps.
6Touch targets 48px minimum, 64px for list rows. Inputs 16px.
7No emojis (Lucide icons only). No em dashes or double hyphens in any copy.
